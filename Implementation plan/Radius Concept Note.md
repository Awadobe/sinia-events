# Radius Concept Note

## Product thesis

Radius is an event discovery and operations platform designed for organizations, independent organizers, and attendees in Sierra Leone. It replaces fragmented workflows built from flyers, Google Forms, spreadsheets, ticket links, and manual check-in lists with one connected experience: discovery, registration, tickets, communication, check-in, reporting, and organization pages.

## Target users

- Visitors who want to discover and register for events without being forced to create an account.
- Signed-in attendees who want reliable access to tickets and upcoming registrations.
- Independent organizers and organizations that need to create, manage, and measure events.
- Event collaborators who need limited access to invitations, attendee management, and check-in.
- Radius platform staff who oversee organizations and platform safety.

## Value beyond Google Forms

Google Forms collects answers. Radius manages the full event lifecycle: public discovery, branded event pages, flexible registration questions, confirmation, tickets, QR check-in, organizer collaboration, attendee management, organization followership, analytics, reporting, and future paid ticketing.

## Business model

Radius will use a freemium and transaction-led model suited to a price-sensitive market.

### Free

- Public event creation and discovery.
- Basic custom registration fields.
- Registration management.
- Tickets and QR check-in.
- Basic attendee counts and CSV export.
- Standard Radius branding and a small organizer team.

### Radius Pro

- Advanced and organization-wide analytics.
- Automatic and scheduled reports.
- More organizer seats and richer permission controls.
- Scheduled email, SMS, and WhatsApp reminders.
- Advanced form logic, file uploads, and richer data collection.
- Custom branding, custom domains, and optional removal of Radius branding.
- Certificates, recurring events, and priority support.
- Promotional and audience-growth tools.

### Transaction and usage revenue

- A service fee on paid event tickets, charged only when the organizer earns revenue.
- Pay-as-you-use SMS or WhatsApp message credits.
- Optional featured event and discovery promotion.

## Payment decision

Monime is the planned payment provider for paid ticketing. The product owner already has a Monime account and an existing space. Integration will begin only after the free event workflow is stable and Monime merchant, settlement, webhook, refund, and fee requirements have been confirmed.

## Product sequencing

Adoption comes before monetization. Basic custom fields remain free during beta so organizers can run real events and Radius can learn which advanced capabilities create enough operational or financial value to justify payment.
